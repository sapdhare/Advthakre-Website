FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Understand ESIC compliance and employee benefits.

What is ESIC?

ESIC is a social security scheme providing medical and financial benefits to employees.

Who is eligible for ESIC?

Employees earning within prescribed salary limits are eligible.

Is ESIC mandatory?

Yes, for establishments meeting employee threshold requirements.

What benefits does ESIC provide?

Medical care, maternity benefits, disability support, and insurance.

What is ESIC contribution rate?

Employee contributes 0.75% and employer contributes 3.25%.

Do you handle ESIC notices?

Yes, we provide complete legal support for ESIC compliance and notices.
