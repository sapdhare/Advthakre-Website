FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Understand your legal rights in family and divorce matters.

What are the types of divorce in India?

Divorce can be mutual (both parties agree) or contested (one party files based on legal grounds under applicable laws).

Who gets child custody after divorce?

Custody is decided based on the best interest of the child, considering emotional, educational, and financial factors.

What is maintenance or alimony?

Maintenance is financial support provided to a spouse or child under laws such as Section 125 CrPC.

Can divorce cases be settled without court?

Yes, mutual divorce and settlements can be resolved through agreement and legal documentation.
