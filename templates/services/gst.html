FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Understand GST compliance and legal requirements.

What is GST?

GST is an indirect tax on supply of goods and services in India, replacing multiple taxes.

Who needs GST registration?

Businesses crossing turnover threshold or involved in interstate trade must register.

What happens if GST returns are not filed?

Penalties, interest, and legal notices may be issued.

What is input tax credit (ITC)?

ITC allows businesses to reduce tax liability on inputs.

Can GST registration be cancelled?

Yes, under certain conditions as per GST rules.

Do you handle GST notices?
