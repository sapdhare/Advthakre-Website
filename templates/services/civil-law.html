FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Get clarity on common civil law matters before proceeding with legal action.

What is civil law?

Civil law deals with disputes between individuals or organizations involving property, contracts, money recovery, and legal rights. It focuses on resolving issues through compensation or court orders.

What types of cases fall under civil law?

Civil law includes property disputes, contract disagreements, recovery of money, landlord-tenant issues, and family-related civil matters.

How long does a civil case take?

The duration depends on the complexity of the case, evidence, and court procedures. Some cases may resolve quickly, while others may take longer due to hearings and appeals.

Do I need a lawyer for civil disputes?

Yes, professional legal guidance ensures proper documentation, strong representation, and better chances of a favorable outcome in court.

Can civil cases be settled outside court?

Yes, many civil disputes are resolved through negotiation, mediation, or settlement, which saves time and legal costs compared to full court proceedings.

What should I do if someone breaches a contract?

You should consult a legal expert immediately. You may file a civil suit for damages, compensation, or enforcement of the contract.
