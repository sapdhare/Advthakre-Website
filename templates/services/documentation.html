FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Understand the importance of proper legal documentation.

Why is legal documentation important?

It ensures clarity, legal enforceability, and protection from disputes.

Are verbal agreements legally valid?

Some are valid, but written agreements are always recommended for proof and enforceability.

Do all agreements need registration?

Certain agreements like property transactions must be registered under law.

What is stamp duty?

A tax paid on legal documents to make them legally valid.

Can I draft legal documents myself?

While possible, professional drafting ensures legal accuracy and avoids future risks.

What happens if a document is not legally valid?

It may not be enforceable in court, leading to legal complications.
