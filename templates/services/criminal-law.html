Legal Framework

Governed by Criminal Laws in India

Our services strictly comply with Indian criminal laws, ensuring strong legal defense and fair judicial process.

Indian Penal Code (IPC), 1860

Defines criminal offenses and penalties under Indian law.

Criminal Procedure Code (CrPC), 1973

Outlines procedures for investigation, trial, and arrest.

Indian Evidence Act, 1872

Governs admissibility of evidence in court proceedings.

Special Criminal Laws

Includes cybercrime laws, NDPS Act, and other special provisions.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Get clarity on criminal law procedures and legal rights.

What is criminal law?

Criminal law deals with offenses against individuals or the state, involving punishment such as fines or imprisonment.

What should I do if I am arrested?

Stay calm and contact a lawyer immediately. You have the right to legal representation.

What is bail?

Bail allows temporary release from custody while the case is ongoing, subject to court conditions.

Can criminal cases be settled?

Some cases can be compounded or settled depending on the nature of the offense.
