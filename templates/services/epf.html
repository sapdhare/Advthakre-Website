FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Understand EPF compliance, employee benefits, and legal requirements.

What is EPF and who is eligible?

EPF (Employees’ Provident Fund) is a retirement benefit scheme under the EPF Act, 1952. It applies to employees earning within prescribed limits and working in registered establishments.

Is EPF registration mandatory for employers?

Yes, businesses employing 20 or more employees must register under EPF as per statutory requirements.

What is UAN (Universal Account Number)?

UAN is a unique identification number assigned to each employee to manage EPF contributions across multiple employers.

How are EPF contributions calculated?

Both employer and employee contribute 12% of basic salary and dearness allowance towards EPF, as per EPFO guidelines.

What happens if EPF compliance is not followed?

Non-compliance may lead to penalties, interest, and legal action under EPF Act provisions.

Can EPF amounts be withdrawn?

Yes, EPF can be withdrawn under specific conditions such as retirement, unemployment, or medical emergencies as per EPFO rules.
