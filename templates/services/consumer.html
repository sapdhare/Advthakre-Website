FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Understand your rights as a consumer under Indian law.

What is consumer law?

Consumer law protects buyers from unfair trade practices, defective goods, and deficient services under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

Who can file a consumer complaint?

Any consumer who has purchased goods or services and faced loss or unfair treatment can file a complaint.

Where can I file a consumer case?

Cases can be filed in District, State, or National Consumer Commissions depending on the claim value.

What compensation can I claim?

You can claim refund, replacement, compensation for loss, and damages for mental harassment.

Is a lawyer required for consumer cases?

Not mandatory, but legal assistance improves chances of success and proper case handling.

How long does a consumer case take?

Duration depends on case complexity, but consumer forums aim for faster resolution compared to regular courts.
